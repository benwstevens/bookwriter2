VOICE AND TONE:
Third person, warm and plain. A patient explainer who likes the subject and
wants the reader to be able to repeat the idea at dinner. No showing off.

TARGET AUDIENCE:
A curious adult with no science background. Assume intelligence, assume no
vocabulary. Define each technical word once, in the sentence where it appears.

READING LEVEL:
Eighth grade. Short sentences for the key ideas. Paragraphs of two to four
sentences.

STRUCTURE:
Follow the chapter structure in the outline. Use descriptive subheadings.
Stay within 10 percent of the word target.
